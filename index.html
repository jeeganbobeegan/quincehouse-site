Quince House is a 19th century brick Victorian in Salt Lake City's Marmalade District, holding two furnished homes for monthly stays. Original woodwork, tall windows, and old brick run through both, a short walk from the Capitol and downtown. Rent includes all utilities and internet, each home has in-unit laundry, and each has two off-street parking spaces in the private driveway and back parking area. Six ski resorts in Park City and the Cottonwood canyons sit about 35 to 45 minutes away, and the airport is 12 minutes. Each home has its own application below.

Marmalade District

A small historic neighborhood on the west slope of Capitol Hill, named for its streets: Quince, Apricot, Almond, and other fruit trees. The blocks are lined with 19th century brick and adobe homes.

It is quiet and residential, with coffee and dinner a few minutes' walk away, downtown below, and six ski resorts within about 45 minutes.
